About Me

I am a Civil Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET), Bangladesh, with a strong focus on Environmental and Water Resource Engineering.

My research interests center on computational approaches to environmental and water resource challenges, particularly groundwater modeling, contaminant transport, machine learning, remote sensing, and GIS.

I am particularly interested in leveraging computational tools and environmental data to understand complex pollution processes and support sustainable water resource management.
