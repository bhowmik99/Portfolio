About Me

I am a Civil Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET), Bangladesh, with a strong focus on Environmental and Water Resource Engineering.

My research interests center on computational approaches to environmental and water resource challenges, particularly groundwater modeling, contaminant transport, machine learning, remote sensing, and GIS.

I am particularly interested in leveraging computational tools and environmental data to understand complex pollution processes and support sustainable water resource management.

Research Experience

Long-Term Chromium Migration Modeling

Water Practice and Technology | 2026 | First Author

Modeled long-term groundwater flow and chromium contaminant transport from the Rajbandh open landfill using MODFLOW-2005 and MT3DMS to assess the spatial and temporal migration of chromium in the groundwater system.

Methods: MODFLOW-2005 · MT3DMS · ModelMuse · ArcGIS

Spatial and Temporal Prediction of Heavy Metal Concentrations

Ongoing Research | Bangladesh River Sediments

Developing a computational framework to predict heavy metal concentrations in Bangladesh river sediments by integrating laboratory measurements with environmental predictors derived from Google Earth Engine and supervised machine learning.

Methods: Google Earth Engine · Random Forest · Python · GIS
