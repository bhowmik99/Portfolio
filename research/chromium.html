Modeling of Long-Term Chromium Migration from the Rajbandh Open Landfill

Water Practice and Technology | IWA Publishing | 2026

Overview

This research investigated the long-term migration of chromium from the Rajbandh open landfill and assessed its potential impact on the surrounding groundwater system.

Research Objective

The study aimed to model groundwater flow and chromium transport over the long term and assess the spatial expansion of the contaminated area.

Modeling Framework

  • MODFLOW-2005 was used for groundwater flow modeling.
  • MT3DMS was used for chromium contaminant transport simulation.
  • ModelMuse was used as the graphical modeling environment.
  • ArcGIS was used for spatial analysis and visualization.
