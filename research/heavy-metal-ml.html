Spatial and Temporal Prediction of Heavy Metal Concentrations in Bangladesh River Sediments

Ongoing Research | 2010–2025

Overview

This research aims to forecast heavy metal concentrations in Bangladesh's river sediments by integrating laboratory measurements with environmental and socioeconomic predictors.

Research Objective

The primary objective is to investigate the relationship between land-use change, climate variability, population density and laboratory-measured heavy metal concentrations.

Dataset

  • 720 laboratory observations from 48 major rivers.
  • Historical observations covering 2010–2024.
  • Target metals include Pb, Cr and Cd.

Environmental Predictors

  • Annual rainfall.
  • Urban and industrial land-use percentages.
  • Population density.

Machine Learning Framework

  • Supervised regression.
  • Random Forest Regressor.
  • 80/20 train-test strategy.
  • 2024 data used as a final validation dataset.

Expected Outcomes

  • Predicted heavy metal concentrations for 2025.
  • Spatial hotspot maps for contaminated river systems.
  • A computational framework supporting environmental monitoring and pollution management.
