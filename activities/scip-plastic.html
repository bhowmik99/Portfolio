Volunteer — SCIP Plastic Project (KUET Part)

Overview

SCIP Plastics is a collaborative German-Bangladeshi research project focused on reducing land-based plastic pollution entering the Bay of Bengal. Running from 2021 to 2025, the project aims to strengthen long-term, knowledge-based waste management capacities in Bangladesh. A central achievement is the establishment of a Knowledge Transfer Hub with a dedicated Waste Lab – the first of its kind on a university campus in Bangladesh. This hub now serves as a platform for applied research, training, and capacity building in sustainable waste management. Field research is conducted in Khulna, Mongla Port, and Chattogram Port, where the team analysed local waste systems and implemented selected countermeasures. In cooperation with the local municipality, an inner-city Awareness Centre was set up to support research, education, and community engagement.
