Vice President — Sylhet Association, KUET

Overview

Founded in 2005 to unite Sylheti students and foster a supportive community, the association promotes Sylhet's distinct culture and language across campus, notably by organizing an annual campfire event. Since 2022, it has expanded its mission to humanitarian response, actively mobilizing, collecting, and distributing emergency relief during Sylhet's frequent flash floods. My Responsibilities • Cultural Event Management: Organized the 2023 annual campfire event to showcase and celebrate Sylheti cultural heritage within the campus community. • Emergency Relief Operations: Led collection and coordination efforts for relief supplies during the 2022 Sylhet flash floods to support affected local communities.
