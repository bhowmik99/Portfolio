Assistant Coordinator of KUET Gym Association

Overview

Founded in 2024 to support the growing fitness community at KUET, the association oversees campus gym operations. It manages machinery maintenance, requisitions new fitness equipment, enforces facility rules, schedules the campus swimming pool, and organizes sports and fitness competitions. My Responsibilities (Assistant Coordinator) • Equipment Procurement: Researched student needs and recommended cost-effective gym machinery aligned with the KUET Student Welfare Center's budget. • Maintenance & Operations: Tracked equipment repairs and facility maintenance needs, reporting actionable data directly to university authorities for swift resolution.
